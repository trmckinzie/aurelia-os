# 👤 Richard Dawkins
---

### 📝 Profile & Context
> Dawkins clarified the logic of natural selection through a gene-centered lens, providing conceptual tools that strongly support my interest in evolutionary explanations of behavior, cognition, and cooperation.

### 📚 Key Works (In Vault)
* [[The Selfish Gene]]
* The Extended Phenotype
- The Blind Watchmaker
- Climbing Mount Improbable
- River out of Eden
- The God Delusion

### ⚛️ Core Concepts
- Gene-Centered View of [[Evolution]]** — Selection understood at the level of replicators.
- **[[Replicator]]** — Entities that make copies of themselves across generations.
- **[[Survival Machines]]** — Organisms as vehicles for genes.
- **Extended Phenotype** — Gene effects extending beyond the body.
- **Blind Watchmaker** — Natural selection as a non-teleological designer ([[Non-Teleological Process]]).
- **Kin Selection** — Altruism explained via shared genes.
- **Reciprocal Altruism** — Cooperation emerging through repeated interactions.
- **Cumulative Selection** — Small changes accumulating into complexity.
- **[[Memes]]** — Cultural analogs of genes.
