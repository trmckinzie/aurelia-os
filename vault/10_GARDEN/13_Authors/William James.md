# 👤 William James

### 📝 Profile & Context
> **The Father of American Psychology** and a titan of American Philosophy. Born into an elite intellectual family (brother to novelist Henry James), William trained as a physician (MD, Harvard) before pivoting to physiology, then psychology, and finally philosophy. His career tracks the evolution of the field itself: moving from the biological roots of the brain (1870s) to the phenomenological study of the mind (1890s), and finally to the metaphysical nature of truth and reality (1900s).

He is historically significant for mediating the conflict between **Structuralism** (rigid analysis of mental parts) and **Functionalism** (adaptive purpose of mental processes). Personally, he is defined by his conquering of severe depression through the philosophical acceptance of **Free Will** ("My first act of free will shall be to believe in free will").

### ⚛️ Core Concepts
- [[Stream of Consciousness]] — _The idea that thought is continuous, flowing, and changing, not a set of discrete "bricks."_
    
- [[James-Lange Theory]] — _The theory that physiological arousal precedes and causes the emotional experience (we are afraid because we run)._
    
- [[Functionalism]] — _The view that mental states are defined by their causal roles and survival value, not their internal constitution._
    
- [[Pragmatism]] — _The philosophical stance that the "truth" of a belief is determined by its practical consequences and utility._
    
- [[Habit]] — _The concept of neural plasticity as the "flywheel of society," automating behavior to free up cognitive resources._
    
- [[Radical Empiricism]] — _The belief that the relations between things (like "being next to") are just as real and directly experienced as the things themselves._
    
- [[The Empirical Self]] — _The tripartite division of the self into the Material Me, the Social Me, and the Spiritual Me._
