# 👤 Steven Pinker

### 📝 Profile & Context
> Pinker is a central figure in modern cognitive science who integrates [[Psychology]], [[Linguistics]], and evolutionary theory to explain language, [[Mind]], and human behavior in ways that align closely with my interests in [[Cognition]], [[Evolution]], and clear scientific thinking. ([[Rationality]])

### 📚 Key Works (In Vault)
- The Language Instinct
- [[How the Mind Works]]
- [[The Blank Slate]]
- Words and Rules
- The Better Angels of Our Nature
- Enlightenment Now
- Sense of Style

### ⚛️ Core Concepts
- Language Instinct —[[ Language]] as an evolved, biologically grounded human capacity.
- Modularity of Mind — The mind composed of specialized, evolved mechanisms.
- Psycholinguistics — Scientific study of how language is represented and processed.
- [[Evolutionary Psychology (NotebookLM)]] — Explaining [[Cognition]] and behavior through adaptive function.
- Anti-Blank Slate — Rejection of the view that the mind is infinitely malleable.
- Innateness — Inborn cognitive structures shaped by [[Evolution]].
- Mental Computation — Mind as an information-processing system.
- Violence Decline Thesis — Argument that violence has decreased over historical time.
- Cognitive Biases — Systematic deviations from [[Rationality]] rooted in mental architecture.
- Scientific Humanism — Defense of reason, science, and Enlightenment values.
