# 👤 Leda Cosmides

### 📝 Profile & Context
> A pioneering American psychologist who, alongside her husband John Tooby, essentially founded the modern field of Evolutionary Psychology. Operating out of UC Santa Barbara, Cosmides argues that the human mind is not a general-purpose computer, but rather a "Swiss Army Knife" of highly specialized, biologically hardwired modules. Through the lens of our vault, she proves that our cognitive "software" was written to solve specific survival problems in the Pleistocene era, not to navigate modern civilization.
