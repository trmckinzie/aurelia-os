# 👤 John Tooby

### 📝 Profile & Context
> An American anthropologist and the co-founder of Evolutionary Psychology alongside Leda Cosmides. While Cosmides brought the cognitive psychology framework, Tooby provided the deep anthropological and evolutionary logic. He famously defined and critiqued the "Standard Social Science Model" (SSSM)—the prevailing academic assumption that the human mind is a "blank slate" shaped entirely by culture. Tooby argued that culture is an _emergent property_ of our biological hardware, not the other way around.
