**📝 BRIEF SUMMARY:**
>Amygdala + PFC interplay --> The PFC regulates and inhibits signals sent from the amygdala and other limbic areas in order to encourage actions that favor a long term goal over a short term initiative or biological urge. The amygdala can also affect the judgement (computation) of the PFC as seen in cases of emotional priming and motivated reasoning. Although the PFC has the capacity to inhibit biological urges in favor of more complex, long term plans, it is the most complex computational machinery in the brain, which makes it metabolically expensive. If the brain is fatigued the PFC may physically lack the energy to fire its inhibitory signals. The brain defaults to cheaper, faster hardware such as limbic areas to take over when the PFC is offline.
