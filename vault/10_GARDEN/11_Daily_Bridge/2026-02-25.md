**📝 BRIEF SUMMARY:**
>This article explores various strategies for learning and demonstrates that re reading and highlighting text are ineffective ways of learning. These low friction, recognition activities produce the fluency illusion but do not actually lead to long term quality learning outcomes. Instead, learners should design their learning habits with the goal of engaging with desirable difficulties. The best practices for learning are by using various retrieval techniques and free recall summaries. The text also talks about [[Interleaving]], which forces the learner to have to switch between the kinds of problems the have to solve which makes the process more difficult, thus increasing the [[Germane Cognitive Load]], which is necessary for deep learning.
