**📝 BRIEF SUMMARY:**
>The 1956 information processing symposium brought together numerous researchers such as [[Newell & Simon]], [[Noam Chomsky]], [[Nat Rochester]], [[G.C. Szikali]]. [[George Miller]] identified that this symposium marked the conception of the field Cognitive Science. What followed in the coming decades was the formal development of the interdiciplanary field now known as [[Cognitive Science]]. This field encompasses [[Neuroscience]], [[Linguistics]], [[Psychology]], [[Computer Science]], [[Philosophy of Mind]], and [[Anthropology]]. Chomsky was particularly important in bringing Linguistics to the party
