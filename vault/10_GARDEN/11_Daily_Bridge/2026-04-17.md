**📝 BRIEF SUMMARY:**
>The second lecture title "The Wisdom of Impermanence" on Joseph Goldtein's lecture series on the Waking Up app focuses on a simple yet powerful insight about impermanence. He mentions a story about a student asking a teacher to sum up Buddhism in a phrase. That phrase was something like "Buddhism is recognizing that everything changes". This is not an esoteric truth, yet we forget that indeed everything is in a constant flux. Joseph specifically talks about how we generally experience the world at the level of concepts, which is of course adaptive and natural but that it is crucial to see things from the perspective of a higher resolution reality. Similar to viewing a movie at the level of pixels on a screen, one ought to view objects of experiences at  a more granular sensory level. It is not the case that because I have an "arm or leg" at the concept level from day to day, but instead that my perception of my "arm" is constantly changing. All experiences are transitory, and mindfulness is about recognizing this truth of impermanence at all levels. The same way that my immediate experience is constantly changing, so are my cells at a microscopic level, so is the state of my local community, my country, societal change over long periods of time, all the way up to the birth and death of stars, galaxies, and the entire universe at large. Everything is always changing, can I be mindful of this truth?
