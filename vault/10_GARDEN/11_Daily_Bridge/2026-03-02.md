[[Rene Descartes]] dualism was the dominant perspective on the relationship betwene mind and body but this idea has proven insufficient. I for example align more closely with the idea that the mind is a byproduct of the brain and that the physical brain state determines the quality of the mind. Consciousness itself is still a mysterious phenomenon but I find it useful to remind myself about the relationship between the physical brain and the subjective qualia that is affected by changes at the physical level.
