**📝 BRIEF SUMMARY:**
>Today in class we began discussing the prevalence and importance of cognitive biases. Cognitive biases can be adaptive or maladaptive depending on the context in which the bias is being examined. Overconfidence can be very adaptive in the sense that a confident perspective and attitude may yield social and performance benefits. On the other hand, humans naturally commit illogical errors as a result of overconfident thoughts, actions, beliefs. [[Daniel Kahneman]] identifies [[Overconfidence]] as the underlying principle that connects all forms of cognitive bias. Our inability to consider [[Bayesian Probability]], is directly related to our reliance on affective and intuitive assumptions.
