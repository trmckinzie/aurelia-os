**📝 BRIEF SUMMARY:**
>When analyzing modern human behavior, it is important to recognize the evolutionary mismatch between modern environments and those that resemble the [[EEA]]. Many [[EPM]]s were "designed" by evolution to solve stone age problems. In ancestral environments, conditions were much harsher, food was scarce, and strangers were to be considered threats to ones tribe. Cultural evolution is a much faster process than biological evolution which creates the situation modern humans have to deal with, the [[Evolutionary Mismatch]]. 

>Sapolsky also provides important nuance on the mechanics of social hierarchy (mostly in Baboons) such as the insight that the impact of [[Social Hierarchy]] depends on stability and coping mechanisms. For example ones social rank may determine that subordinates experience higher levels of [[Sustained Stress]] compared to higher ranked individuals in a stable hierarchy. This contrasts with a situation where an Alpha may experience heightened levels of stress if the stability of the social hierarchy is at risk. This means that social rank modulates the release of certain [[Hormone]]s, for example higher ranked individuals may interpret an ambiguous situation as a threat to their rank (release of [[Testosterone]]), meanwhile a subordinate may release [[Cortisol]] in response to submission.
