**📝 BRIEF SUMMARY:**
>The difference between pre and post synaptic changes is that changes at the pre stage involve the "volume" of the chemical message itself. Post synaptic changes are about the sensitivity of the "microphone" in detecting the signal. Non linear excitation explains an exponential spike in a biological response when a specific electrical threshold is reached. This mechanism is the barrier to entry for irrelevant experiences so that they do not lead to long term learning, instead it requires deep focus and learning to rewire neural circuitry in the long term. This long term learning is explained by long term potentiation which refers to long term strengthening of synaptic connections due to recent patterns of activity. Long term depression happens when inactivity leads to persistent weakening of synaptic connections. Myelination is the biological process where specialized glial cells wrap a fatty, insulating layer (the myelin sheath) around the axon (the "cable") of a neuron.
