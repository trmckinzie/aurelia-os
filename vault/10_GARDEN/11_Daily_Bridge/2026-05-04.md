**📝 BRIEF SUMMARY:**
>The CNS is composed of the brain and spinal cord. It is responsible for the processing of all sensory information, voluntary movement, and higher order cognitive functions. ANS is the peripheral nervous system which is involved in the regulation of subconscious bodily functions such as heart rate, blood pressure, and digestion. The ANS connects the nervous system to the organs. PNS is rest and digest & SNS is fight ort flight. Neuroplasticity refers to the nervous systems ability to change, grow, become "rewired" and pruned. Functional plasticity refers to the remapping of neural connections for new or improved functions while structural plasticity refers to changes in the physical mass and amount of neurons. Neurogenesis is the specific process referring to the growth of new neurons via neural stem cells. More neurons equals more computational capacity. The nervous system naturally seeks a state of homeostasis through the process of allostasis. The allostatic load refers to the biological wear and tear endured by the physical body as a result of exposure to stress, damage, and other entropic forces. Dopamine is a neurotransmitter involved in desire, anticipation of reward, motivation, and goal directed behavior. It is highly concentrated in the reward circuitry of the brain and is largely produced by the nucleus accumbens / substantia nigra. Dopamine spikes or crashes as a result of reward prediction error. The brain responds with a surge of dopamine for unexpected rewards, reinforcing, crediting, and motivating a certain behavior while dopamine levels crash in response to a disappointment. High allostatic load kills cognitive bandwidth and low levels of dopamine (in response to a crash or chronic cheap dopamine which adjusts baseline) are essential concepts related to the Economics of Cognitive Bandwidth.
