# 🧠 Evolutionary Biology & Behavioral Endocrinology

### 🧐 Definition (The Scope)
>The integrated study of how ancient evolutionary pressures shaped the physical hardware of the human brain, and how hormones (endocrinology) act as the chemical messengers executing those survival algorithms in real-time. This discipline views human behavior not as a spontaneous psychological choice, but as a biological output driven by genetic legacies, environmental triggers, and neurochemical states. It is the foundation of the "Wetware Stack."

### 🧪 Unresolved Questions

- **The Limits of Free Will:** To what exact biological extent can the Prefrontal Cortex (metacognition) permanently override ancient endocrine responses (like the amygdala's fear response), versus merely suppressing them temporarily?
    
- **Nature vs. Nurture Epigenetics:** How rapidly does environmental context (culture, trauma, diet) physically rewrite the expression of ancient genes within a single lifetime?
