# 🧠 Evolutionary Psychology

### 🧐 Definition (The Scope)
>The theoretical approach to psychology that attempts to explain useful mental and psychological traits—such as memory, perception, or language—as adaptations, i.e., as the functional products of natural selection.

**The Central Thesis:** The human brain is not a general-purpose computer that can learn anything equally well. It is a collection of specialized software programs ([[EPM]]) designed by [[Natural Selection]]to solve the specific problems our ancestors faced during the Pleistocene era ([[EEA]]). **The Motto:** "Our modern skulls house a Stone Age mind."

---

### 🔑 Core Concepts (The Bricks)
- **[[EEA]]:** This is not a specific place (like the African Savanna) but a statistical composite of the environmental pressures that shaped our species over the last 2 million years. We are adapted to _that_ world, not the modern world of iPhones and skyscrapers.
    
- **[[Mismatch Theory]]:** The friction caused when Stone Age software runs in a Space Age environment.
    
    - _Example:_ We evolved to crave high-calorie foods (sugar/fat) because they were rare in the EEA. Today, they are abundant, leading to an obesity epidemic. The adaptation (sugar craving) has become maladaptive.
        
- **[[Parental Investment Theory]] (Robert Trivers):** The theory that the sex investing _more_ in offspring (females: 9 months + nursing) will be the "Choosier" sex, while the sex investing _less_ (males: minutes) will be the "Competitive" sex. This explains vast swathes of human mating behavior.
    
- **[[Kin Selection]] (Inclusive Fitness):** The evolutionary strategy that favours the reproductive success of an organism's relatives, even at a cost to the organism's own survival.
    
    - _Hamilton's Rule ($rB > C$):_ I will jump in a river to save 2 brothers or 8 cousins, because mathematically, that saves 100% of my genes.

### 📚 Foundational Texts
- **[[The Adapted Mind]]** by **Barkow, Cosmides, & Tooby** (The 1992 academic "bible" that defined the field).
    
- **[[The Selfish Gene]]** by **[[Richard Dawkins]]** (Shifted the focus from the "individual" to the "gene" as the unit of selection).
    
- **[[The Evolution of Desire]]** by **David Buss** (The definitive text on human mating strategies).
    
- **[[How the Mind Works]]** by **[[Steven Pinker]]** (A masterclass in reverse-engineering the mind as an engineered system).
### 🧪 Unresolved Questions
- The "Just-So" Story Problem:_ How do we prove a behavior is a genuine adaptation and not just a "Spandrel" (a byproduct) or cultural noise? Critics argue Evo Psych invents plausible stories without hard fossil evidence.
    
- The Rapid Evolution Debate:_ Have we evolved significantly _since_ the Stone Age? (e.g., Lactose tolerance evolved in just a few thousand years). Are we more "modern" than Evo Psych admits?
