# 📖 How the Mind Works

**👤 Author:** [[Steven Pinker]]
 Full Notes:

---

### 💡 The Core Argument (Thesis)
> The mind is a system of organs of computation, engineered by natural selection to solve the problems faced by our foraging ancestors. Pinker's synthesis joins two theories that are usually kept apart: the **computational theory of mind** (mental life consists of information processing) and **natural selection** (complex design without a designer). Neither alone explains a mind; together they explain why we have the specific faculties we have — vision, language, kinship, emotion — rather than a single all-purpose reasoning engine.

---

### 🧠 Concepts Extracted
* **[[Computational Theory of Mind]]:** Thinking is computation. Beliefs and desires are information, realized physically, causing behavior by their form.
* **[[EPM]]:** The mind as a set of specialized modules — the Swiss Army knife rather than the blank slate.
* **[[Natural Selection]]:** The only known process that produces functional complexity, and therefore the only available explanation for mental design.
* **[[Adaptation]]:** Pinker is careful about what counts as one, and explicitly treats music, art and religion as candidates for *by-products* rather than adaptations.
* **[[EEA]]:** The environment the machinery was tuned for, which is not the one it now runs in.
* **[[Perception]]:** The long central treatment of vision as an inverse-optics problem — recovering a world from an ambiguous retinal image.
