# 📖 The Principles of Psychology

**👤 Author:** [[William James]] 
 Full Notes: 

---

### 💡 The Core Argument (Thesis)
>Psychology is the "Science of Mental Life, both of its phenomena and of their conditions."

**The Shift:** Before this book, psychology was largely a branch of philosophy or a rigid cataloging of "sensations" (Structuralism). James argued that the mind is not a static building made of bricks (ideas), but a flowing river (process). He treated the mind as a biological organ adapted for survival—the birth of **Functionalism**.

**The Scope:** It is encyclopedic, covering everything from brain anatomy and instinct to hypnosis, will, and the perception of time. It famously asserts that consciousness is personal, changing, continuous, and selective.

---

### 🧠 Concepts Extracted
- **[[Stream of Consciousness]]:** James attacked the idea that thought is made of discrete "bits." He argued thought is a continuous flow.
    
    - _The Metaphor:_ "A river or a stream are the metaphors by which it is most naturally described... let us call it the stream of thought, of consciousness, or of subjective life."
        
    - _Flights and Perchings:_ Thought has resting places (substantive parts, e.g., the image of a "Cat") and rapid movements between them (transitive parts, e.g., the feeling of "and" or "if").
        
- **[[Habit]]:** He viewed habit as "the enormous flywheel of society," keeping us in our bounds.
    
    - _Plasticity:_ He was one of the first to propose that the nervous system is "plastic"—that repeated actions physically carve pathways in the brain, making future actions easier.
        
- **[[The Self]]:** He divided the "Me" into three parts:
    
    1. **Material Self:** Your body, clothes, and family.
        
    2. **Social Self:** The recognition you get from mates (we have as many social selves as we have groups of friends).
        
    3. **Spiritual Self:** Your inner subjective being.
        
- **[[James-Lange Theory]] of Emotion:** The counter-intuitive idea that physiological arousal comes _before_ the emotional experience.
    
    - _Standard View:_ See Bear $\rightarrow$ Feel Fear $\rightarrow$ Run.
        
    - _James's View:_ See Bear $\rightarrow$ Run (Heart pounds) $\rightarrow$ Brain interprets body's reaction as "Fear."
        
    - _Quote:_ "We feel sorry because we cry, angry because we strike, afraid because we tremble."

### 📝 Key Insight

- **Attention is Choice:** James famously defined attention as "the taking possession by the mind, in clear and vivid form, of one out of what seem several simultaneously possible objects or trains of thought."
    
    - This implies **Free Will** (or at least the feeling of it) lies in the ability to _choose_ what to attend to. By selecting which part of the stream to focus on, we construct our own reality.
