# 📖 The Adapted Mind

**👤 Author:** [[John Tooby]]
 Also edited by [[Leda Cosmides]] and Jerome Barkow. Full Notes:

---

### 💡 The Core Argument (Thesis)
> The founding document of evolutionary psychology as a research programme. Its central move is to reject the **Standard Social Science Model** — the assumption that the mind is a general-purpose learning device and that culture explains content — and replace it with the claim that the mind contains many domain-specific mechanisms, each shaped by selection to solve a particular adaptive problem. The methodological payoff is that adaptive problems can be specified in advance, which turns evolutionary psychology into a way of *generating* hypotheses about cognitive design rather than only explaining findings after the fact.

### 🧠 Concepts Extracted
* **[[EPM]]:** Massive modularity — many special-purpose mechanisms, not one general reasoner.
* **[[EEA]]:** Tooby and Cosmides' own term. Not a place or a period, but a statistical composite of the selection pressures that built a given adaptation.
* **[[Adaptation]]:** The strict standard for calling a trait one, and the distinction from by-products and noise.
* **[[Kin Selection]]:** Inclusive fitness as the basis for the psychology of family, and a worked example of the method.
* **[[Cheater Detection]]:** The Wason selection task result — reasoning performance jumps when an identical logical problem is framed as policing a social contract.
* **[[Natural Selection]]:** The engineering constraint underneath all of it.
