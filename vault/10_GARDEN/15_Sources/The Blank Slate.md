# 📖 The Blank Slate

**👤 Author:** [[Steven Pinker]]
**🔑 Zotero Key:** **📍 Full Notes:** On reMarkable

---

### 💡 The Core Argument (Thesis)
>Pinker argues that the “blank slate” view of human nature is empirically false and intellectually damaging, and that recognizing evolved psychological predispositions is essential for understanding mind, behavior, morality, and social institutions without undermining human values. 

---

### 🧠 Concepts Extracted
*(Concepts linked here will show in Graph View)*
- [[Blank Slate]]— The claim that the human [[Mind]] has no innate structure and is entirely shaped by experience.
- Innate Psychological Mechanisms — Evolved mental structures that constrain learning and behavior.
- Evolutionary Psychology — Framework explaining cognition and behavior via adaptive functions.
- Anti-Determinism Clarification — Innateness does not imply inevitability or moral fatalism.
- Nature–Nurture Interaction — Traits emerge from interaction between biology and environment, not one alone.
- Moral Psychology — Moral intuitions as products of evolved cognitive systems.
- Tabula Rasa Politics — How blank-slate thinking distorts views on equality, responsibility, and social policy.
- Biological Constraints — Limits on cultural and social engineering imposed by human nature.
- Fear of [[Biology]] — Resistance to evolutionary explanations due to moral and political concerns.
- Scientific [[Humanism]] — Reconciling a biological view of mind with dignity, rights, and progress.
