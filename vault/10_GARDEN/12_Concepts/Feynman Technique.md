# ⚛️ [[Feynman Technique]]

**🔗 Related:** [[Thinking Tool]], [[Analogy]]

---

### 💡 Definition
> A learning method for achieving deep understanding by explaining a concept in simple, clear language as if teaching it to a novice, thereby revealing gaps in knowledge.

### 📝 Key Insight
* Difficulty explaining a concept plainly is a reliable signal of incomplete understanding.
- Simplification forces the learner to replace memorization with causal and conceptual clarity.
- Iterating between explanation and revision strengthens long-term retention and transfer.
