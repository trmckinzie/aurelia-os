# ⚛️ Cortisol

**🔗 Related:** [[Hormone]], [[HPA]], [[Sustained Stress]], [[Allostatic Load]], [[The Amygdala]], [[Evolutionary Mismatch]], [[Social Hierarchy]], [[Robert Sapolsky]]

**⚡ Contrasts With:** <!-- optional, notes that complicate or push back on this one -->

---

### 💡 Definition
> The primary glucocorticoid stress hormone in humans, released by the adrenal cortex at the end of the **[[HPA]]** axis cascade. Its job is to mobilize energy for an emergency: it raises blood glucose, sharpens short-term vigilance, and shuts down every process the body can afford to defer — digestion, growth, reproduction, immune response, and the slow work of tissue repair. Cortisol is not a "bad" chemical. It is an exquisitely well-designed solution to a problem that lasts ninety seconds.

### 📝 Key Insight
* **Built for a sprint, billed for a marathon:** The entire cortisol response assumes the stressor is acute and physical — a predator, a fall, a fight. Under those conditions the shutdown of long-term maintenance is free, because the emergency resolves before the deferred bills come due. Chronic psychological stressors keep the same machinery switched on for months, which is what turns an adaptation into damage. This is the mechanism underneath **[[Allostatic Load]]**.
* **The bandwidth tax:** Through the lens of "The Economics of Cognitive Bandwidth," sustained cortisol is a direct levy on **[[The Prefrontal Cortex (PFC)]]**. Elevated glucocorticoids strengthen **[[The Amygdala]]** while degrading hippocampal function and prefrontal control — the brain is literally rebalanced toward fast, reactive threat-detection and away from planning. Stress does not just make deep work feel harder; it reallocates the hardware away from it.
* **Rank, not adversity, sets the baseline:** Sapolsky's baboon field work found that the biological cost of **[[Social Hierarchy]]** depends on *predictability and control*, not on workload. In a stable hierarchy, subordinates carry the highest baseline cortisol. In an unstable one, the dominant animal does. Powerlessness is the variable, which is why an unread inbox can be more corrosive than hard physical labor.
* **The mismatch, precisely:** **[[Evolutionary Mismatch]]** is often described loosely. Cortisol is where it becomes concrete and measurable — the same cascade evolved to survive a leopard now fires at an email, and the physiological consequence is identical because the body has no way to tell the difference.
