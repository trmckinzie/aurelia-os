# ⚛️ Neuroenergetics

**🔗 Related:** [[The Prefrontal Cortex (PFC)]], [[System 1 vs System 2 (Dual-Process Theory)]], [[Nonlinear Excitation]], [[Long Term Depression (LTD)]], [[Myelination]], [[Working Memory]]

---

### 💡 Definition
> The study of the cellular processes that produce and consume energy (in the form of ATP, derived from glucose and oxygen) to maintain brain function.

- **The 20% Rule:** Despite making up only about 2% of a human's total body weight, the brain consumes a massive 20% of the body's resting metabolic energy. The vast majority of this energy is spent simply maintaining the electrical resting potential of neurons and powering the sodium-potassium pumps after a neuron fires.
    
- **The Biological Ledger:** Every single thought, memory retrieval, or moment of focus requires a physical transaction of ATP. The brain does not have infinite batteries; it is strictly constrained by how fast it can deliver blood, oxygen, and glucose to specific neural regions (known as the hemodynamic response)

### 📝 Key Insight
- **The Literal Economics of Bandwidth:** Through the lens of "The Economics of Cognitive Bandwidth," Neuroenergetics is the exact, physical accounting system of the brain. When you experience "mental fatigue" after a long study session, it is not just a psychological feeling—it is the localized depletion of metabolic resources, most often in the hyper-expensive **[[The Prefrontal Cortex (PFC)]]**.
    
- **The Hardware Justification:** This metabolic scarcity explains almost every structural law of your Wetware Stack. **[[System 1 vs System 2 (Dual-Process Theory)|System 1]]** exists specifically because it uses heavily **[[Myelination|myelinated]]** pathways, which require a tiny fraction of the ATP to fire compared to raw gray matter. **[[Long Term Depression (LTD)]]** is the brain ruthlessly pruning unused synapses to avoid paying the metabolic "maintenance tax." The brain is the ultimate biological cheapskate, sacrificing accuracy and logic for energy efficiency at every possible opportunity.
