# ⚛️ Systems

**🔗 Related:** [[Emergence]], [[Feedback Loops]], [[Entropy]], [[Black Box Theory]], [[Holism vs. Reductionism]], [[Network Theory]], [[Cybernetics]]

---

### 💡 Definition
> > A set of interacting or interdependent component parts forming a complex/intricate whole. The term comes from the Greek _systēma_ ("organized whole"). A system is defined not just by its parts, but by the **relationships** between those parts and the **boundary** that separates it from the environment.
> 
> **The Universal Model:**
> 
> Almost all systems, regardless of field, can be modeled with three steps:
> 
> 1. **Input:** Energy, Matter, or Information enters.
>     
> 2. **Process:** The system transforms the input.
>     
> 3. **Output:** The result is exported back to the environment.
>     

### 🔭 Macrofield Perspectives

|**Field**|**How it views a "System"**|**Key Insight**|
|---|---|---|
|**Physics**|**Thermodynamic Container**|Focuses on the **Boundary**. Is it _Isolated_ (nothing in/out), _Closed_ (only energy in/out), or _Open_ (energy + matter in/out)? This determines the system's fate regarding **Entropy**.|
|**Biology**|**Autopoietic Machine**|Focuses on **Self-Preservation**. Biological systems are "Autopoietic" (self-creating). They actively maintain a specific internal state (**Homeostasis**) despite external chaos.|
|**Cybernetics**|**Control Loop**|Focuses on **Regulation**. It views systems as information processors governed by **Feedback Loops**. The goal is to minimize error between the "Goal State" and the "Actual State."|
|**Sociology**|**Emergent Network**|Focuses on **Interactions**. A society is a system where the "Whole" (Culture/Economy) has properties that the "Parts" (Individuals) do not possess.|
|**Engineering**|**Black Box**|Focuses on **Function**. Often treats the internal process as irrelevant ("Black Box") as long as the Input consistently produces the desired Output.|

### 📝 Key Insight
* **Emergence (The Magic Trick):** The defining characteristic of a system is **Emergence**.

- A water molecule is not wet. "Wetness" is an _emergent property_ of a _system_ of water molecules.
    
- A neuron is not conscious. "Consciousness" is an _emergent property_ of a _system_ of neurons.
    
- **The Axiom:** "The whole is greater than the sum of its parts." You cannot understand the system by merely disassembling it (Reductionism); you must study the interactions.
