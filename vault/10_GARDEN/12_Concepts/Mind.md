# ⚛️ Mind

**🔗 Related:** [[Consciousness]], [[The Hard Problem]], [[Dualism vs. Monism]], [[Computational Theory of Mind]], [[Qualia]], [[Embodied Cognition]], [[Self-Concept]]

---

### 💡 Definition
> The complex of faculties involving cognitive aspects such as thinking, intelligence, memory, and attention, as well as non-cognitive aspects such as emotion and instinct.

- **The Central Mystery:** It is the subjective screen upon which reality is projected. While we can objectively measure a _brain_ (2.5 lbs of wet tissue), the _mind_ is the private, internal experience of being that brain.
    
- **The Core Question:** What is the relationship between the physical brain and the mental mind? (The Mind-Body Problem).

### 🔭 Perspectives Across the Disciplines

| **Field**                | **The Metaphor**             | **How it views the "Mind"**                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| ------------------------ | ---------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Philosophy (Dualism)** | **The Ghost in the Machine** | Historically (Descartes), the mind was seen as a non-physical substance (Soul/Res Cogitans) interacting with the physical body. Most modern philosophers reject this for **Physicalism**, but the intuition remains. |
| **Cognitive Psychology** | **The Software**             | Views the mind as an **Information Processing System**. If the brain is the Hardware (Silicon/Neurons), the mind is the Algorithm (Windows/Cognition) running on it.                                                 |
| **Neuroscience**         | **The Activity**             | "The mind is what the brain _does_." There is no "ghost"; there is only the firing of neurons. Mental states are identical to physical brain states (Identity Theory).                                               |
| **Buddhism/Eastern**     | **The Stream**               | The mind is not a static "thing" or a "self," but a momentary, fluctuating stream of consciousness (_Citta-santāna_). It is a process, not an object.                                                                |
| **Embodied Cognition**   | **The Pilot**                | The mind is not just in the head; it extends into the body and the environment. You "think" with your hands, your tools, and your spatial surroundings.                                                              |
### 📝 Key Insight
* **The "Hard Problem" (Chalmers):** We can explain the _Easy Problems_ of the mind (how we focus attention, how we store memories) by mapping them to neural circuits.

- But we cannot explain the **Hard Problem**: _Why does it feel like something?_
    
- Why is the processing of light accompanied by the subjective experience of "Redness" (**Qualia**)? A robot could detect red light without _feeling_ red. The existence of this subjective interiority remains the greatest gap in our scientific model of the universe.
