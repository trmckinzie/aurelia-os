# ⚛️ Entropy

**🔗 Related:** [[Second Law of Thermodynamics]], [[Negentropy]], [[Information Theory]], [[The Arrow of Time]], [[Maxwell's Demon]], [[Free Energy Principle]]

---

### 💡 Definition
> A measure of the number of possible microscopic configurations that correspond to a macroscopic state.

- **In Thermodynamics (Boltzmann):** It is a measure of **Disorder** or randomness in a closed system. Nature tends to move from Order (Low Entropy) to Disorder (High Entropy).
    
- **In Information Theory (Shannon):** It is a measure of **Uncertainty** or "Surprise."
    
    - **Low Entropy:** A text message that says "aaaaaaaaa" (Highly predictable, low information).
        
    - **High Entropy:** A truly random password (Highly unpredictable, maximum information).

### 📝 Key Insight
* - **The Arrow of Time:** Entropy is the only physical law that is not time-reversible. Newton's laws work the same running forward or backward, but the Second Law of Thermodynamics dictates that entropy _must_ increase over time in an isolated system. This gives the universe a direction (Past $\to$ Future).
    
- **Entropy = Hidden Information:** In physics, "disorder" really just means "information we don't have." If you know the position and velocity of every gas molecule, the "entropy" vanishes. Therefore, entropy is a measure of our _ignorance_ about the micro-state of a system.
