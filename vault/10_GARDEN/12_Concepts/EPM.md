# ⚛️ EPM

**🔗 Related:** [[EEA]], [[Massive Modularity]], [[Natural Selection]], [[Mismatch Theory]], [[Sexual Selection]], [[Cheater Detection]]

---

### 💡 Definition
> A set of processes inside an organism that evolved to solve a specific problem of survival or reproduction recurrently faced by ancestors.

**The Anatomy of an EPM:**

1. **Exists because of Success:** It exists in current humans only because it solved a specific problem (e.g., "Find food" or "Avoid snakes") better than alternative designs in the ancestral past.
    
2. **Input Criteria:** It is designed to take in only a narrow slice of information (e.g., the eye is an EPM that accepts light but ignores radio waves).
    
3. **Algorithmic Processing:** It transforms that input via decision rules (If $\to$ Then).
    
4. **Output:** It produces a physiological activity, information for other mechanisms, or manifest behavior (e.g., "Run away").

### 📝 Key Insight
* **The Interactionist Fallacy:** Critics often argue "Is it Nature or Nurture?" An EPM perspective says this is a false dichotomy.

- An EPM (Nature) _requires_ specific environmental input (Nurture) to function.
    
- Example: Calluses are an EPM. You are genetically designed to produce calluses (Nature), but you only actually get them if you experience friction (Nurture). The mechanism is innate; the outcome is environmental.

### 🧩 The "Swiss Army Knife" Model

- **Domain Specificity:** EPMs are not general-purpose problem solvers. They are specialized tools.
    
    - There is no "General Problem Solver" in the brain just as there is no "General Organ" in the body. You have a heart for pumping and a liver for filtering.
        
    - Similarly, you have a **Cheater Detection Module** for social exchange, a **Language Acquisition Device** for grammar, and a **Fear Module** for predators.
        
    - The mind is a collection of these specialized "blades," like a Swiss Army Knife, rather than a single [[Blank Slate]]. [[The Blank Slate]]
