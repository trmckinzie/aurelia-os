# ⚛️ Hebbian Learning

**🔗 Related:** [[Neuroplasticity]], [[Long-Term Potentiation (LTP)]], [[Synapse]], [[Dendrite]], [[Connectionism]], [[Donald Hebb]]

---

### 💡 Definition
> A theory in neuroscience that proposes an explanation for the adaptation of neurons in the brain during the learning process. It describes a basic mechanism for **synaptic plasticity**, where the biological weight of a connection between two neurons increases when they fire simultaneously.

**The Famous Mnemonic:**

> _"Neurons that fire together, wire together."_

**The Inverse:**

> _"Neurons out of sync, lose their link."_ (Often referred to as Long-Term Depression or LTD).

### 📝 Key Insight
* **The Brain as a Statistician:** Hebbian learning proves the brain is essentially a correlation machine. It doesn't "know" what causes what in a philosophical sense; it simply assumes that if two things happen at the same time repeatedly, they must be related. This is the biological basis for both **Learning** and **Superstition/Phobias**.

### 🧬 The Biological Mechanism

In 1949, Donald Hebb proposed that if Neuron A repeatedly takes part in firing Neuron B, some growth process or metabolic change takes place in one or both cells such that Neuron A's efficiency, as one of the cells firing B, is increased.

1. **Persistence:** It isn't just a one-time spark. It requires repeated or persistent stimulation.
    
2. **Causality:** Neuron A must be contributing to the firing of Neuron B.
    
3. **Physical Change:** This results in **Long-Term Potentiation (LTP)**. The "receiving" dendrite might sprout more receptors, or the "sending" axon might release more neurotransmitters.
    

### 🤖 The Computational Impact (Connectionism)

Hebbian Learning is the bedrock of **[[Connectionism]]**. It moved AI away from "Top-Down" logic (if-then statements) toward "Bottom-Up" associations.

- **Associative Memory:** This explains why the smell of a specific perfume can instantly trigger a memory of a person. Because the neural pattern for the "Smell" and the neural pattern for the "Person" fired together in the past, they are now "wired" together. Activating one automatically triggers the other.
    
- **Artificial Neural Networks:** In machine learning, we simulate this by adjusting the **Weights** between nodes. If a specific input consistently leads to a correct output, the "weight" (connection strength) is increased.
