# ⚛️ Evolutionary Brain Stratification

**🔗 Related:** [[Evolutionary Mismatch]], [[The Limbic System]], [[The Prefrontal Cortex (PFC)]], [[The Central Nervous System (CNS)]], [[System 1 vs System 2]], [[The Amygdala]]

---

### 💡 Definition
> The physical architectural reality of the brain, demonstrating how it was built sequentially over millions of years of evolution. Instead of designing a new brain from scratch for humans, evolution simply stacked new, complex hardware on top of older, primitive hardware.

**The Triune Heuristic:** Often modeled (heuristically, if not perfectly anatomically) as the "Triune Brain," it consists of three distinct layers operating simultaneously.
    
- **Layer 1 (The Reptilian Brain):** The ancient brainstem and cerebellum. It strictly handles autonomic, life-sustaining functions (breathing, heart rate, physical balance) with zero conscious thought.
    
- **Layer 2 (The Mammalian Brain):** **[[The Limbic System]]** (including **[[The Amygdala]]**). Layered over the brainstem, it introduced rapid emotional processing, memory, and social hierarchy evaluation.
    
- **Layer 3 (The Primate Brain):** The Neocortex, crowned by **[[The Prefrontal Cortex (PFC)]]**. The newest, outermost layer responsible for logic, abstract thought, impulse control, and long-term planning.

### 📝 Key Insight
- **The Architecture of Conflict:** Through the lens of "The Economics of Cognitive Bandwidth," stratification is the physical reason human beings experience internal conflict. You are not a single, unified computer; you are three distinct biological operating systems crammed into one skull, often fighting for control of the same physical body.
    
- **The Hardware Mismatch:** When you experience an **[[Evolutionary Mismatch]]**, it is literally Layer 2 (ancient, paranoid, and metabolically cheap) aggressively overriding Layer 3 (modern, logical, but easily exhausted). **[[System 1 vs System 2|System 1]]** is the dominant, rapid-fire voice of the older, deeper layers, while **[[System 1 vs System 2 (Dual-Process Theory)|System 2]]** is the fragile, energy-hungry voice of the newest cortical layer trying desperately to maintain executive control.
