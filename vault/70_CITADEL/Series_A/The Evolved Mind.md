*Answer these 5 questions to find the "Soul" of the video before researching.*

**1. The Symptom:**
*What specific pain, confusion, or "glitch" does the viewer feel regarding this topic?*
> For this episode I will look at Health, Wealth, and Wisdom as broad goals of human beings. When it comes to these things there are intuitive approaches to how we attain such states of being healthy wealthy and wise and why they matter. These intuitions are viscerally powerful and are reinforced by common assumptions about the mind and its function. The specific pain I will address is the inability for humans to come to terms with the most plausible theoretical account of why we have minds, how they came to be through Evolution by natural selection, and the functional purpose they serve us as biological organisms. Mismatch theory is one important theory that explains why our minds don't always serve us well in a modern context. This episode will define evolution, natural selection, survival machines, replicators, the mind, evolutionary psychology and related foundational concepts to the evolutionary naturalism worldview.

**3. The Shift:**
*If this theory is true, what assumption must be shattered?*
> Mind body dualism is intuitive but implausible in a modern context. This challenges traditional religious assumptions and as Dennett explains in DDI, this worldview is quite dangerous to your cherished assumptions about life and meaning, but you must decide whether you value truth or ignorance. There are real tradeoffs involved in making this decision. In many ways you are better off not coming to terms with what you are because ignorance truly can be blissful. This bliss comes with a terrible cost, and a reliance on skyhooks. You can live the rest of your life under a spell, that your mind, personality, behavior, and entire life is explained by ancient ontological frameworks and that you are a moral agent destined for an afterlife of Heaven or Hell. This comes with its own convenience and struggle but it is inherently a simpler narrative to accept. On the other hand, you can accept the evolutionary naturalist worldview. 

**4. The Wisdom:**
*How does understanding this elevate the human experience?*
> Initially an internalization and acceptance of this new ontology, will likely shatter your worldview which is accompanied by a great feeling of distress. You will need to rebuild your worldview, and in many ways you are deciding to take the red pill on the nature of the human mind when you start to explore and endorse Evolutionary Psychology. You may lose friends, you may face a bout of depression, and you may be tempted to become nihilistic about this realization. This channel has a particular goal in mind which is to always diagnose a problem that we as humans may be dealing with, to assess the problem with the best possible science and philosophy I have encountered, and to provide practical advice for dealing with the harsh realities of human existence with an emphasis on the positive insights that can be derived from the evolutionary naturalism worldview.

*What is the single, concrete action the viewer must take immediately?*

The single action is to decide which pill you want, because I promise that at the end of a painful transition from the traditional dualistic / superstitious worldview, you will eventually find deep insights about how the evolutionary psychological worldview can serve your pursuit of a healthier, wealthier, and wise life.
