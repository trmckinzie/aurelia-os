**📝 BRIEF SUMMARY:**
>Todays focus was on macro-level concepts that provide important context to the wetware stack for week 1. Evolutionary Brain Stratification refers to the structural and functional organization of the brain as an evolved organ. The triune heuristic is useful although not entirely accurate in explaining the structure of the brain. The brainstem and cerebellum are often referred to as the reptilian brain or hindbrain and these areas are responsible for autonomic functions such as breathing and balance subconsciously. The next level up is referred to as the mammalian brain and is composed of the limbic system (Amygdala, Hypothalamus, Hippocampus, Basal Ganglia). The functions of these areas involve emotions, motivations, and memory. The 3rd layer is the primate brain or neocortex. This region is responsible for higher order cognitive functions, long term planning, complex decision making, inhibition control and language.
